How TRACIDUS Thinks

1

Linguistic Triage

The deterministic layer extracts observable signals; contextual interpretation is kept separate from the mathematical decision layer.

2

Technical Enrichment

Extracted URLs are enriched with technical threat intelligence while the psychological layer remains independent and explainable.

3

Evidence Correlation

Technical evidence is compared with psychological signals so TRACIDUS can distinguish convergence from uncertainty.

4

Human-Centric Report

The result separates technical evidence, psychological interpretation, behavioral inference, uncertainty, and actionable guidance.
